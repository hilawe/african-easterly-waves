# Derived per-trough tables

These tables are regenerated by `scripts/run_canonical.py` (seed 0), which runs
`scripts/build_deposit.py` first and then the downstream producers in a fixed order.
Running `build_deposit.py` alone rebuilds the per-trough, per-case and per-parcel tables
but leaves the model and sensitivity tables from a previous generation, so the whole
sequence is the reproducible route.

`canonical_registry.csv` is the table every number in the paper is audited against, and
it is the one to read first. `canonical_numbers.csv` is the contrast-and-point subset
written by `build_deposit.py`, so it is smaller and does not carry the model,
wave-estimand or sensitivity rows the registry adds.

Clock convention. All times are relative to trough passage at the sampling meridian. The
environment sample sits at -24 h and the 48 h back-trajectories extend the record to
-72 h. Column suffixes `m24` .. `m72` are passage-relative hours.

Class labels. MCS-active and MCS-quiet are the upper and lower terciles of the forward
mesoscale-convective response within each 10-degree longitude by calendar-month cell
(minimum 30 troughs per cell). Activity refers to continental convection within 24 h of
trough passage, never to tropical cyclogenesis.

Tiers. `dev` is the 2000-2004 design sample, `heldout` the 20 untouched seasons
(1983-1999 and 2005-2007), `pooled` the primary 25-season record (1983-2007).

## canonical_numbers.csv

One row per reported statistic.

- `tier`; `level` (700, 850, or empty for the column/shear family); `statistic`;
  `time_rel_h` (passage-relative hours); `unit`; `kind` (contrast or point)
- `n_quiet`, `n_active` (observations); `clusters_quiet`, `clusters_active` (waves)
- `mean_quiet`, `mean_active`, `diff` (active minus quiet), `ci_lo`, `ci_hi`
  (wave-cluster bootstrap 95 percent interval), `significant`
- `note` (estimator details; box placement, conditionality, threshold)

## troughs_<tier>.csv

Every JAS corridor trough observation (5-20 N, 30 W to 40 E): `traj_id` (wave), `time`,
`lat`, `lon`, `year`, `month`, `response` (forward CS-245 count), `label`.

## cases_<tier>_<level>.csv

One row per trough observation in the stratified split. Identifiers as above, plus:

- `box_rh_m24`: the Eulerian box sample
- `rh_m24`..`rh_m72` and `thetae_m24`..`thetae_m72`: along-inflow case means
- `mix_*` and `T_*`: the vapor/temperature decomposition
- `frac_south`..`frac_local`: origin-sector parcel fractions
- `dlat_origin`: meridional displacement of the origin, degrees
- `exceed_p70`: the -72 h inflow sample exceeds the tier's pooled 70th percentile
- `antecedent`: prior-day CS-245 count (700 hPa table only)

## paths_<tier>_<level>.csv

Group-mean inflow path in trough-relative coordinates, one row per trajectory hour per
class: `elapsed_h` (hours before the seed), `time_rel_h` (passage-relative), `group`,
`mean_rel_lon` (mean parcel longitude minus trough longitude), `mean_lat`,
`n_parcels`. Drawn as the mean inflow paths in the supply-contrast figure.

## parcels_<tier>_<level>.csv

One row per back-trajectory parcel (9 per case): `case`, `parcel`, `traj_id`,
`seed_time`, `seed_lat`, `seed_lon`, `origin_lat`, `origin_lon` (position at -72 h),
`sector` (displacement-based origin class; `lost` where the parcel left the domain or
record).

## canonical_registry.csv

The audited superset of `canonical_numbers.csv`. It merges the contrast and point rows
above with the model, wave-estimand and sensitivity rows written by the downstream
producers, and it is what `tools/number_audit.py` checks the manuscript against. Same key
columns as `canonical_numbers.csv`, plus `irr`, `irr_lo`, `irr_hi` and `pvalue` for the
model rows.

## eligible_troughs.csv

Every corridor trough that passed the complete-window rule, before the model's
complete-case filter: `wave`, `time`, `lon`, `response`, `inflow_rh`. This is the cohort
the wave-unit estimands use, which is wider than the model's sample.

## control_model_design.csv

The control model's fitted sample, one row per eligible trough whose six predictors are
all finite: `time`, `lon`, `response`, `inflow_rh`, `box_rh`, `antecedent`, `amplitude`,
`shear`, `tcwv`, `lonmonth`, `year`, `wave`. Written after the complete-case filter, so
it is a subset of `control_model_design_eligible.csv`.

## control_model_design_eligible.csv

The same columns over every ELIGIBLE trough, so predictors may be missing. It is the
reuse cache for `scripts/control_model.py`, which validates it against
`troughs_pooled.csv` and `cases_pooled_700.csv` before reusing it and rebuilds otherwise.
The published design table above is derived from it on every run.

## within_between_model.csv

The common-sample within-between decomposition of the inflow-moisture association:
`component` (`between_wave`, `within_wave`, `equality`), `irr`, `irr_lo`, `irr_hi`,
`pvalue`, and `chi2` on the equality row.

## Other model and sensitivity tables

`control_model_coeffs.csv`, `control_model_ladder.csv`, `control_model_panel.csv`,
`control_model_predictor_corr.csv` and `control_model_sensitivities.csv` carry the
control model's tier fits, the control ladder, the full standardized panel, the
predictor correlation matrix and the negative-binomial dispersion sensitivity.
`wave_estimands.csv`, `response_sensitivity.csv`, `yearstrat_sensitivity.csv`,
`leadlag_stats.csv`, `fig3_descriptive_stats.csv`, `null_r1_fig2_stats.csv` and
`attrition_<tier>.csv` carry the wave-unit estimands, the response-definition and
year-stratification sensitivities, the lead-lag statistics, the Fig. 3 descriptive peaks,
the Fig. 2 randomization result and the per-tier attrition ledger.
